CREDITS
=======

Vinyl crackle sounds
--------------------
vinylstart1.mp3, vinylstart2.mp3 and vinylstart3.mp3 are based on the sounds
"vinyl start crackle 1", "vinyl start crackle 2" and "vinyl start crackle 3" by
lulyc, from the pack "Vinyl start and end crackle sounds" on Freesound:

  Author:  lulyc  https://freesound.org/people/lulyc/
  Pack:    https://freesound.org/people/lulyc/packs/19233/
  Sounds:  https://freesound.org/s/339447/  (start crackle 1)
           https://freesound.org/s/339452/  (start crackle 2)
           https://freesound.org/s/339451/  (start crackle 3)
  License: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 (CC BY 3.0)
           https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/

Changes made: the original WAV files were converted to smaller MP3 files. The game
also plays them louder or softer while it runs. Nothing else was changed.

Christmas songs
---------------
The carols (Jingle Bells, We Wish You a Merry Christmas, Joy to the World,
Hark! The Herald Angels Sing and O Tannenbaum) are traditional tunes that are in
the public domain. They are played by the game itself with a bell sound made in
the browser.

Jump scare sounds
-----------------
jumpsound01.mp3 to jumpsound05.mp3 are jump scare sound effects from Pixabay:

  Source:  https://pixabay.com/sound-effects/search/jumpscare/

Pictures
--------
The character, present and scare pictures were made with an AI image tool
(Google). Santa Claus, elves, reindeer, snowmen and gingerbread men are
traditional figures.

What the Apache-2.0 license covers
----------------------------------
The license in LICENSE covers the game's own code (index.html, game.js,
style.css) and the pictures. The sounds named above keep their own licenses.
