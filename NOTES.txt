CHRISTMAS PRESENT GAME - NOTES
==============================
Made by Dylan. Claude helps only when asked and builds only what Dylan specifies.

THE IDEA
--------
Two players take turns choosing presents. One present has a bomb. A safe present
says "You are safe!" and shows a joke, then disappears. If you open the bomb you
get a jump scare.

CHARACTERS (chosen at the start)
--------------------------------
Santa Claus, an elf, a gingerbread man, a snowman, the Grinch, Rudolph
(Rudolph added later; picture is Rudolph.jpeg with a capital R).
(Not decided yet: does the character do anything, or is it just a picture?)

RULES
-----
- 16 presents in each round (was 12; changed to 16 in a 4x4 grid).
- 3 rounds.
- Players take turns. Player 1 goes first in round 1, Player 2 in round 2,
  Player 1 in round 3.
- Safe present: shows "You are safe!". The present goes away.
  Not every safe present shows a joke: each time, there is a random chance that
  a joke is shown too (1 in 2). A joke never repeats within a round.
- Bomb opened: jump scare, and the OTHER player gets a point.
- Only one present left in a normal round: it is the bomb. Both players get a
  point and nobody opens it.
- After round 3 the higher score wins.
- If the score is tied: SUDDEN DEATH round.
  * It is a normal round (same presents, same jokes, new random bomb).
  * A spin-the-wheel picks who goes first. The wheel is ONLY used here.
  * The last present counts as a bomb, so whoever has to open it gets the scare
    and the other player wins.

PLAY AGAINST THE BOT (added after the floating home screen)
-----------------------------------------------------------
The home screen has two buttons: "Play with a friend" (two players, as before)
and "Play against the bot". With the bot, only one player picks a character and
types a name; the bot is always Player 2 and gets a random character and a fun
name (Jingle-Bot, Tinsel, Sparky, Beepo, Frosty-Bot, Bolt), shown like
"Bolt (Grinch)". The bot has no clue where the bomb is: after a pause of about
1.5 seconds (BOT_THINK_MS in game.js) it picks a random present that is still
there. Your clicks are ignored while it is the bot's turn. If the bot opens the
bomb, the jump scare still plays and you get the point. Rounds, scoring, the
"one present left" rule and sudden death (with the wheel) all work the same.
"Play again" keeps the same mode (bot or friend).
