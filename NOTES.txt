CHRISTMAS PRESENT GAME - NOTES
==============================
Made by Dylan. Claude helps only when asked and builds only what Dylan specifies.

THE IDEA
--------
Two players take turns choosing presents. One present has a bomb. A safe present
says "You are safe!" and shows a joke, then disappears. If you open the bomb you
get a jump scare.

CHARACTERS (chosen at the start)
--------------------------------
Santa Claus, an elf, a gingerbread man, a snowman, the Grinch.
(Not decided yet: does the character do anything, or is it just a picture?)

RULES
-----
- 12 presents in a normal round.
- 3 rounds.
- Players take turns. Player 1 goes first in round 1, Player 2 in round 2,
  Player 1 in round 3.
- Safe present: shows "You are safe!". The present goes away.
  Not every safe present shows a joke: each time, there is a random chance that
  a joke is shown too (1 in 2). A joke never repeats within a round.
- Bomb opened: jump scare, and the OTHER player gets a point.
- Only one present left in a normal round: it is the bomb. Both players get a
  point and nobody opens it.
- After round 3 the higher score wins.
- If the score is tied: SUDDEN DEATH round.
  * It is a normal round (same presents, same jokes, new random bomb).
  * A spin-the-wheel picks who goes first. The wheel is ONLY used here.
  * The last present counts as a bomb, so whoever has to open it gets the scare
    and the other player wins.

-----------
1. Row of present buttons ............ DONE (4x3 grid, 12 presents)
2. Random bomb + "You are safe!" joke .. DONE (1 in 2 joke chance, no repeats
   in a round; bomb currently just shows the word "BOMB!" as a placeholder)
3. Turns and points .................. DONE (Player 1 / Player 2 names for now;
   score and whose turn shown above the presents; round locks when it ends,
   by the bomb or by one present left; no next round starts yet)
4. 3 rounds, then sudden death on a tie . DONE (a "Next round" button after
   rounds 1-2; after round 3 the higher score wins, or a tie shows "Start
   sudden death"; a "Play again" button after the game. In sudden death there
   is no skip rule, the bomb opener loses, scores don't change. Who goes first
   in sudden death is a RANDOM PLACEHOLDER until the wheel (step 7) is built.)
5. Character select .................. DONE (Player 1 picks, then Player 2;
   both may pick the same character. The character's name replaces "Player 1/2"
   everywhere and its picture shows next to the score. If both pick the same
   one the names get " 1" / " 2". Character select shows again on Play again.
   Characters don't have any special power.)
6. Jump scare ........................ DONE, WAITING FOR DYLAN'S GHOUL IMAGE
   (full screen the instant the bomb opens, shakes and flashes red for 2
   seconds, then the round result shows. Sound is a scream + thump made in
   code (no file), volume SCARE_VOLUME in game.js. The picture is expected at
   ghoul.jpeg; change GHOUL_IMAGE at the top of game.js if the file has another
   name. Until the file exists the scare shows a flashing red screen.
   Image is cropped to fill the screen, so a wide picture loses its sides on a
   tall phone screen. Plays in sudden death too; NOT on the "one present left"
   point, since nobody opens a bomb there.)
7. Spin-the-wheel for sudden death ... DONE (after a tied round 3 a "Spin the
   wheel" button opens the wheel screen: 8 small slices alternating red
   (player 1) / green (player 2) with each player's character picture, and a
   Spin button. The game picks the winner at random first, then animates the
   wheel to land on one of the winner's slices (about 4 seconds). Then it says
   "<name> goes first!" and a "Start sudden death" button begins the round.)
