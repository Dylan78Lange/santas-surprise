CHRISTMAS PRESENT GAME - NOTES
==============================
Made by Dylan. Claude helps only when asked and builds only what Dylan specifies.

THE IDEA
--------
Two players take turns choosing presents. One present has a bomb. A safe present
says "You are safe!" and shows a joke, then disappears. If you open the bomb you
get a jump scare.

CHARACTERS (chosen at the start)
--------------------------------
Santa Claus, an elf, a gingerbread man, a snowman, the Grinch, Rudolph
(Rudolph added later; picture is Rudolph.jpeg with a capital R).
(Not decided yet: does the character do anything, or is it just a picture?)

RULES
-----
- 16 presents in each round (was 12; changed to 16 in a 4x4 grid).
- 3 rounds.
- Players take turns. Player 1 goes first in round 1, Player 2 in round 2,
  Player 1 in round 3.
- Safe present: shows "You are safe!". The present goes away.
  Not every safe present shows a joke: each time, there is a random chance that
  a joke is shown too (1 in 2). A joke never repeats within a round.
- Bomb opened: jump scare, and the OTHER player gets a point.
- Only one present left in a normal round: it is the bomb. Both players get a
  point and nobody opens it.
- After round 3 the higher score wins.
- If the score is tied: SUDDEN DEATH round.
  * It is a normal round (same presents, same jokes, new random bomb).
  * A spin-the-wheel picks who goes first. The wheel is ONLY used here.
  * The last present counts as a bomb, so whoever has to open it gets the scare
    and the other player wins.

NAMES (added after step 7, changed later): after picking a character each player
can type a name (up to 12 letters). The name is OPTIONAL: if it is left blank (or
only spaces, or the same as the character's name) the player is just called after
their character, like "Santa" or "Elf". A typed name shows as "Sam (Santa)"
everywhere: score, turn line, messages, wheel legend. If two players end up with
exactly the same label (for example both blank and both Santa) " 1" / " 2" is
added: "Santa 1" and "Santa 2". The name box says "Leave blank to be <character>".
There is no "go back" button on the name screen yet.

PLAY AGAINST THE BOT (added after the floating home screen)
-----------------------------------------------------------
After pressing Play on the home screen, a "How do you want to play?" screen has
two buttons: "Play with a friend" (two players, as before) and "Play against
the bot". With the bot, only one player picks a character and
types a name; the bot is always Player 2 and gets a random character and a fun
name (Jingle-Bot, Tinsel, Sparky, Beepo, Frosty-Bot, Bolt), shown like
"Bolt (Grinch)". The bot has no clue where the bomb is: after a pause of about
1.5 seconds (BOT_THINK_MS in game.js) it picks a random present that is still
there. Your clicks are ignored while it is the bot's turn. If the bot opens the
bomb, the jump scare still plays and you get the point. Rounds, scoring, the
"one present left" rule and sudden death (with the wheel) all work the same.
"Play again" keeps the same mode (bot or friend).

Screen order now: Home (Play) -> Mode (friend or bot) -> Warning -> Character
select -> Name -> Game. After a game, "Play again" goes back to the Mode screen
("How do you want to play?"), so players can switch between a friend and the
bot. The warning only shows the first time (after that it is skipped).

CHRISTMAS MUSIC (added after the bot)
-------------------------------------
Jolly music starts when the game starts (the first round begins) and plays until
the jump scare. It is silent on the home, mode, warning, character select and
name screens, and it stops again when "Play again" takes players back to the Mode
screen. It is two old public-domain carols, Jingle Bells and We Wish You a Merry
Christmas, played in a loop with a bell sound made in the browser (no music
file). After a scare it stays silent through the round result and comes back when
the next round starts. It keeps playing on the wheel screen (that is mid-game).
(Browsers allow no sound before a click, so the audio is unlocked at the first
Play click, but nothing plays until the game starts.)
A small speaker button in the
top-right corner mutes/unmutes it; the mute choice is only remembered until the
page is reloaded. Volume is MUSIC_VOLUME in game.js; the notes are the JINGLE_BELLS
and WE_WISH_YOU lists.

MORE SCARE SOUNDS (added after the music)
-----------------------------------------
Dylan's five recordings jumpsound01.mp3 .. jumpsound05.mp3 (1.5 to 7.6 seconds
long) are now jump scare sounds, together with the original scream made in code:
six sounds, one picked at random for every scare (list SCARE_SOUNDS in game.js).
Any sound longer than 3 seconds is faded out at 3 seconds (SCARE_SOUND_MAX_SECONDS);
the picture still stays for 2 seconds. They are loaded with a small fetch of the
game's own files (nothing from other sites). If they can't be loaded (for example
the page is opened as a plain file), a normal audio element is used, and if that
fails the scream made in code plays. All five files are recorded at full volume
(some are very loud on average), SCARE_VOLUME (0.7) still applies.

MORE CAROLS AND THE VINYL CRACKLE (added after the scare sounds)
----------------------------------------------------------------
Five carols now play instead of two: Jingle Bells, We Wish You a Merry Christmas,
Joy to the World, Hark! The Herald Angels Sing and O Tannenbaum. All are old
public-domain tunes. Joy to the World, Hark and O Tannenbaum were typed in from
note text on Wikipedia (the Joy tune is from Lowell Mason's 1839 hymnal) and
checked by a script: same notes in the same order, and bar lengths add up. The
songs are in the SONGS list in game.js. Every time the music starts it plays all
five once in a fresh shuffled order (never the same song twice in a row), then
reshuffles. One full round of the playlist is about 2 minutes.
Dylan wanted Deck the Halls, Up on the Housetop, Jolly Old Saint Nicholas as well,
but no reliable note source was found, so they are NOT in yet.
Bug found while testing (fixed before publishing): note names with a flat (Bb)
were not understood, which crashed the whole game script.

Vinyl crackle: at the start of EVERY round (round 1, 2, 3 and sudden death) a
random one of three vinyl "start crackle" sounds plays; the carols start 3 seconds
later (VINYL_LEAD_SECONDS) and the crackle goes on softly under them until it ends.
The music restarts at every round, even if it was still playing from the round
before (a round that ended without a scare). It does not play in the menus, and
not while the speaker button is muted. (Dylan first wanted it only at the start of
a game, then asked for every round.)

WHY AN OLD VERSION SOMETIMES SHOWS UP (browser cache)
-----------------------------------------------------
GitHub Pages tells browsers to keep each file for 10 minutes, so right after an
update a browser can still run the old game.js (this happened several times, for
example the vinyl crackle seemed to play only in round 1 while the new version
plays it every round). Fix for players: Cmd+Shift+R. Fix built in: index.html loads
style.css?v=... and game.js?v=...; CHANGE THE v= VERSION IN index.html WHENEVER
game.js OR style.css CHANGES, so a normal reload picks up the new files.
